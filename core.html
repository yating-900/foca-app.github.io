An interval timer for HIIT, Tabata, circuits and boxing rounds — a big countdown with distinct work and rest colors, audio cues so the phone can stay on the floor, and a clear history of every workout.

Why Core

Set it once. Then just train.

Set the work — by time or by reps — the rest, and how many sets. Save it as a workout and it keeps those settings, ready to start next time. Core counts you through every interval and tells you when to switch, so you're watching your form — not the clock.

Work and rest

Know where you are at a glance.

Work is red, rest is blue, and the next interval is always shown below the countdown — so one look across the room tells you whether to push or breathe.

History & trends

See the work add up.

Every finished session lands in your history. Check today's minutes, your weekly trend and a calendar of training days — with monthly and yearly trends and CSV export in Core Pro.
