A Pomodoro timer with guided stretch breaks

Foca is an ad-free Pomodoro timer for iPhone and Mac that turns every break into a short guided stretch. When a focus session ends, Foca walks you through a stretch for your neck, shoulders, back, hands or legs — with illustrations and voice guidance.

Free to download · No ads · Last updated November 9, 2026

How it works

  1. 1. Focus Start a session — 25 minutes by default, or any length you like.
  2. 2. Take a break When time is up, Foca switches to your break.
  3. 3. Stretch Follow a short illustrated, voice-guided stretch, then start the next round.

Why stretch between Pomodoros?

The Pomodoro Technique already gives you a break every 25 minutes. Most of us spend it scrolling. Standing up for a short stretch is a simple way to use that time to move after sitting — and Foca makes it the default, so you don't have to remember.

What's included

  • Stretches for the neck, shoulders, back, hands, legs and full body, each with an illustration and voice guidance.
  • A stretch button on the timer screen, so you can stretch any time — not only after a session.
  • Ambient sounds for focusing: Forest, Tide, Café, Storm, Bonfire, Library and more.
  • Auto-Run, so focus and break rounds keep going without tapping.
  • Statistics by day, week and category.
