How to Put a Christmas Countdown on Your iPhone Lock Screen

Short answer: iPhone's built-in widgets don't count down to a date, so you need a countdown app with widgets. Create a countdown for Friday, December 25, 2026, then touch and hold your Lock Screen, tap Customize, tap Add Widgets, and choose the app's widget. On iOS 16 or later, the number updates by itself every day.

By Foca Studio · Last updated November 17, 2026

What you need

  • An iPhone on iOS 16 or later. Lock Screen widgets arrived in iOS 16, and Dayfold itself needs iOS 16 or later.
  • A countdown app that offers widgets. This guide uses Dayfold, our countdown and anniversary app, but the iPhone steps are the same for any app.
  • Open the app once after installing it, so iOS lists its widgets.

Step 1: Create the Christmas countdown

In Dayfold:

  1. Open Dayfold and tap the + button. The Create a Moment screen opens.
  2. Name it Christmas. Dayfold recognizes the name (Christmas, Xmas or 🎄 all work) and applies its Christmas theme automatically. In the Emoji card style, the card shows a 3D 🎄.
  3. Set the date to December 25, 2026.
  4. Save.

Prefer to count to Christmas Eve? Set December 24 instead. The steps below don't change.

Step 2: Add the countdown to your Lock Screen

These are Apple's steps for any Lock Screen widget (Apple Support):

  1. Touch and hold the Lock Screen until the Customize button appears, then tap Customize.
  2. Choose the Lock Screen (not the Home Screen) if iOS asks.
  3. Tap Add Widgets below the clock.
  4. Find Dayfold in the list, choose the circular or rectangular widget, and tap or drag it into place.
  5. If you have more than one countdown: on iOS 17 or later, tap the widget and pick Christmas (until you do, it shows your pinned card). On iOS 16, the widget always shows your pinned card, so make sure your Christmas countdown is the pinned one.
  6. Swipe down to close the widget list, then tap Done.

If the row is full, tap the remove button on a widget you don't need to make room.

Step 3 (optional): Add it to your Home Screen

Also from Apple Support:

  1. Touch and hold an empty area of the Home Screen until the apps jiggle.
  2. Tap Edit in the upper-left corner, then Add Widget.
  3. Search for Dayfold, swipe to choose small or medium, and tap Add Widget.
  4. On iOS 17 or later, touch and hold the new widget, tap Edit Widget, and choose Christmas if needed. On iOS 16, it shows your pinned card.
  5. Tap Done.

Lock Screen or Home Screen?

Lock Screen widget Home Screen widget
Where you see it Every time you raise or wake your phone When you unlock and go Home
iOS version iOS 16 or later iOS 16 or later (Dayfold's minimum)
Sizes Circular or rectangular Small or medium
Look Single-tint, matched to your clock and wallpaper: the day count and a calendar icon Full color card
🎄 visible? No, the number only Yes, when the card uses the Emoji style
Best for A glance at "days left" Showing off the card design

Many people use both: a Lock Screen widget for the number, and a Home Screen widget for the 🎄.

If the widget doesn't show up or update

  • Dayfold isn't in the widget list. Open the app once, close it, and try again. Still missing? Restart your iPhone.
  • The number looks a day off. Check Settings → General → Date & Time and make sure your time zone is right. Opening the app also refreshes its widgets.
  • The widget shows a placeholder after a restart. Unlock your iPhone once; widgets can stay hidden until the first unlock.
  • Widgets are hidden while locked. Go to Settings → Face ID & Passcode (or Touch ID & Passcode) and check the options under Allow Access When Locked.
  • It's not ticking every second. That's expected. iOS refreshes widgets on its own schedule; a day count only needs to change once a day.

After December 25

Dayfold starts counting up: the card shows the name followed by "for" and the number of days, and the Lock Screen widget shows "N days ago". You can also delete it and make a New Year countdown next.

FAQ

Can I put a Christmas countdown on my iPhone Lock Screen without an app?

Not as a live number. iPhone's built-in widgets don't count down to a date, so you need a countdown app that offers Lock Screen widgets. A static wallpaper with a number on it won't update.

Does Dayfold have a Christmas theme?

Yes. Name a moment "Christmas" (or "Xmas", or use 🎄) and Dayfold applies its Christmas theme automatically. In the Emoji card style, a 🎄 shows on the card and on Home Screen widgets. Lock Screen widgets show the number only.

Which iPhones support Lock Screen widgets?

Any iPhone that runs iOS 16 or later. You add them from the Lock Screen by touching and holding it and tapping Customize.

Can I count down to Christmas Eve instead?

Yes. Set the date to December 24. Everything else works the same.
