PMDD self-check: common questions

Is this a PMDD test?

It's a self-check, not a diagnosis. It asks about the same things doctors look at for PMDD under the DSM-5 criteria: which symptoms you have in the week before your period, whether they ease once it starts, and how much they get in the way of daily life.

How is PMDD diagnosed?

A doctor looks for at least five symptoms in the week before your period, including at least one of mood swings, irritability, low mood or anxiety, that ease within a few days after it starts and cause real distress or get in the way of daily life. The pattern should be confirmed with daily ratings over at least two cycles.

What's the difference between PMS and PMDD?

Most people who menstruate get some premenstrual symptoms. PMDD is a severe form in which mood symptoms are strong enough to disrupt work, relationships or daily life, and it affects a smaller share of people.

Are my answers saved or sent anywhere?

No. The self-check runs in your browser. Your answers aren't stored or sent to us or anyone else.
