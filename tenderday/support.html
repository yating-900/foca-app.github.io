Tenderday Support

Need help with Tenderday? Email us at foca-2020@outlook.com. It helps to include your iPhone model, iOS version and the Tenderday version (shown at the bottom of the Me tab).

If you're in crisis

If you're in crisis in the US, call or text 988, any time, or visit 988lifeline.org. Tenderday is a diary and can't respond to messages in an emergency.

Common questions

Where is my diary stored?

Only on your iPhone, inside the app. There is no account and nothing is sent to our servers. See the Tenderday Privacy Policy.

How do I move my diary to a new iPhone?

Your diary is part of your iPhone backup. Restore the new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup of the old one. You can also export a CSV copy from Me → Export all my data.

How does Apple Health work with Tenderday?

Tenderday can read the period dates and flow you've logged in Apple Health. It never writes to Health. Turn it on or off in Me → Apple Health → Read period dates. If no days appear, check that Tenderday is allowed to read Menstrual Flow in the Health app's settings for Tenderday.

How do I make a report for an appointment?

Open Insights → Report, pick how many cycles to include and what to show, then tap Export PDF. Your notes are left out unless you turn them on.

The daily reminder doesn't show up

Check that notifications for Tenderday are allowed in Settings → Notifications. The reminder is skipped on days you've already logged.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting the Tenderday app from your iPhone deletes the diary stored in it. Export a copy first if you want to keep one.
