Tenderday Privacy Policy

Tenderday is a private diary for PMS, PMDD and period pain. Your diary stays on your iPhone. We don't have an account system or a server for it, so we can't see what you write.

This policy covers the Tenderday iPhone app. Questions: foca-2020@outlook.com.

What Tenderday stores, and where

Everything you log (how the day felt, period flow, symptom ratings, what you took or did, notes, and notes you keep from the cat) is saved in a database inside the app on your iPhone. There is no sign-in and no Tenderday account.

Tenderday does not sync your diary to our servers or to iCloud. Like other app data, it can be part of your iPhone backup (iCloud Backup or a computer backup) if you have backups turned on. Apple handles those backups under its own terms.

What Tenderday sends over the internet

Nothing. The app has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party code that collects data. It does not send your diary, your Health data or usage data anywhere.

Some links open other apps: Safari for web pages (such as this policy, our support page or 988lifeline.org), and Phone or Messages for the 988 line. What happens there is covered by those services, not by Tenderday.

Apple Health

If you choose to connect Apple Health, Tenderday asks to read one kind of data: menstrual flow. It reads up to the last 24 months to fill in period days in your diary. Tenderday never writes anything to Apple Health. Days you logged, ended or cleared yourself are never overwritten by Health data.

Health data read by Tenderday stays on your iPhone, like the rest of your diary. It is not used for advertising and is not shared. You can turn off access at any time in the Health app or in Settings.

Reminders

If you turn on the daily reminder, Tenderday schedules notifications on your iPhone. They are local notifications: nothing is sent to a server to deliver them.

Exports you share

You can export your diary as a CSV file or a PDF report. Tenderday creates the file in the app's temporary folder and opens the iPhone share sheet. You choose where it goes (for example Files, Mail or AirDrop). Once you close the share sheet, Tenderday deletes its temporary copy, and it also clears any leftover export file the next time the app opens. Notes are left out of the PDF report unless you turn them on.

Once you send or save an export somewhere else, that copy is handled by the app or person you shared it with.

Deleting your data

Because your diary is stored only in the app on your iPhone, deleting Tenderday from your iPhone deletes the diary. Copies in your iPhone backups or in exports you saved elsewhere are not affected.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent or share your data. We never receive it in the first place.

Not medical advice

Tenderday is a personal diary. It records what you log and shows it back to you. It is not medical advice. If you are in crisis in the US, call or text 988 or visit 988lifeline.org.

This website

The foca.work website (including this page) uses Google Analytics to count visits. That is covered by the Foca Studio Privacy Policy. The Tenderday app itself does not use Google Analytics.

Changes

If what the app does with data changes, we will update this page before that version is released, and change the date below.
